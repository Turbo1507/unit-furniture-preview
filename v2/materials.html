Materials and production in Bali

We create furniture with design, climate, daily use and durability in mind

Wood

Most models are built on teak: a dense species that lives outdoors and withstands Bali humidity. Frames use 18 mm plywood and HMR panels, no thin structures.

Where we use it

Teak
Frames of chairs, armchairs and all outdoor furniture
Plywood 18 mm
Frames of beds, sofas, poufs and ottomans
HMR 18 mm, walnut
Bed headboards
Metal
Legs of sofas and outdoor furniture

Fabrics

Textures are chosen by use. Soft for bedrooms and living rooms, heavy-duty for hotels and restaurants, water-resistant for terraces and pools.

Where we use it

Indoor
Soft textures for bedrooms and living rooms
Outdoor
Sun-resistant fabrics for terraces and pool areas
Contract
Heavy-duty fabrics for hotels and restaurants
Cushions
Blue, grey, graphite, grey melange, orange

Fillings

Cushions and upholstery keep their comfort, shape and look even with daily rental turnover and active use

Hardware

Reliable joints and proven hardware, so the furniture lasts and stays comfortable

Designed for tropical humidity, sun and daily wear

  • Sun and outdoorSun-resistant materials for open spaces
  • Everyday useKeeps its look and comfort even under heavy use

How a piece is made

Our own production in Bali: we control every stage, from material selection to final assembly

  • Material selection

    Wood, fabric and filling chosen for the purpose of the model

  • Fabrication

    Cutting and machining on our own equipment

  • Assembly and upholstery

    Frames and upholstery are assembled in our workshop

  • Acceptance

    Geometry, finish, seams and stability checked before shipping
